Breeder Profile

Dr. Roger & Nancy Brown have been actively breeding and showing Ocicats more than twenty years. Retirement from their small animal hospital gave them the time they wanted to provide their Ocicats with a healthy, engaging environment for socialization, play, and bonding.

Dr. Roger Brown served ten years as Director at Large for The Cat Fanciers’ Association.  He continues to serve as Scientific Advisor, and chairs the DNA Program for CFA. He is excited about the continued possibilities in this field. It will continue to provide breeders with the opportunity to breed healthier cats, and help owners maintain the health of their cat.

Pedigree

Appearance

The Ocicat is bred for beauty to emulate the sleek, spotted coats and strong, well-muscled carriage of wild cats.

Temperment

Ocicats are an ideal companion, displaying an adaptable, predictable disposition and agreeable personality.

Health

Thanks to their broad genetic background, Ocicats are not prone to any major health problems and display hybrid vigor and vitality.

Care

Ocicats enjoy the diet of a standard domestic cat and are easy to keep with no special care requirements.
